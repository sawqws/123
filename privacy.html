Forma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Forma is a private, non-commercial Telegram bot that shows its single owner their own WHOOP data (recovery, sleep, strain, workouts, body measurements) and gives training, sleep and nutrition tips.

What data is used

  • WHOOP data you authorize via WHOOP OAuth: recovery, cycles, sleep, workouts, profile (name, email) and body measurements.
  • What you send to the bot: messages, food photos, meals and profile fields (age, height, weight, goal).

How it is used

  • Only to show you your data and generate personal recommendations.
  • To write recommendations, a summary of your data is processed by an AI model (Claude by Anthropic).
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on the owner's private server. WHOOP tokens and keys never leave it.
  • The app inside Telegram is available only to the owner (verified by Telegram signature).

Your control

  • You can revoke access at any time in your WHOOP account settings; the bot then stops receiving data.
  • On request, all stored data is deleted from the server.

Contact

Questions: write to the bot owner in Telegram — @TrainerWhoopbot.
